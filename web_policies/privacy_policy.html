Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 1 August 2026 Last Updated: 31 August 2026 Compliance: Google Play User Data & Indian DPDP Act 2023
Key Summary: OrderKart collects only the basic contact and address details necessary to deliver fresh vegetables, fruits, and essentials to your home. We do NOT collect background GPS location, photos, contacts, audio, or financial payment card numbers.

1. Introduction & Overview

OrderKart ("we", "us", "our") values your trust and is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how OrderKart collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information gathered from users ("you", "your") of the OrderKart mobile application and ordering services. We operate in compliance with the Information Technology Act 2000, IT Rules 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

2. Information We Collect

To fulfill your doorstep delivery requests, we collect limited personal data:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, primary mobile phone number, alternate phone number, and unique Customer Member Code assigned by the store.
  • Delivery Location: House/flat number, road/street name, sub-road, and registered route delivery area in Pune, Maharashtra.
  • Order History: Details of items purchased, quantities, pricing snapshots, order timestamps, and delivery schedules.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens used exclusively to send real-time order status updates and delivery arrival alerts.
  • Technical Access Logs: Standard network request metadata and connectivity status (WiFi/Cellular/Offline) required for application synchronization.

3. Permissions & Data We DO NOT Collect

Our application requests minimal system permissions:

  • No Background GPS Location: Addresses are mapped via predefined store delivery routes; the app does NOT access your device's precise GPS or live movement.
  • No Camera or Media Storage: The app does not access your photos, videos, or internal storage.
  • No Microphone or Bluetooth: We do not request audio or nearby device permissions.
  • No Device Contacts: The app never reads your phonebook.
  • No In-App Payment Card Data: All payments are Pay on Delivery (Cash or on-the-spot UPI QR transfer). We never collect or store debit/credit card numbers or banking PINs.

4. Purpose & Lawful Basis of Processing

Your information is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Assembling, dispatching, and delivering your scheduled farm-fresh produce orders to your doorstep.
  • Transmitting transactional push notifications regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery status.
  • Authenticating your customer account and defending against unauthorized brute-force attempts via automated rate-limiting.
  • Providing customer support and resolving grievance inquiries via WhatsApp and phone helpline.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted, industry-standard technology infrastructure providers bound by confidentiality and security standards:

  • Supabase Inc. (Cloud Database & Authentication): Data is stored in secure cloud servers with TLS 1.3 transit encryption and AES-256 rest encryption.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Dispatches push notifications directly to your device.
  • Google Fonts: Delivers high-quality typography within the mobile interface.

6. Data Security & Storage

All network communications between the mobile application and backend servers are secured via modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption. Authentication tokens are saved on your device using hardware-backed secure storage (Android KeyStore / EncryptedSharedPreferences). We enforce strict row-level security policies on our database.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You have the full right to delete your online customer account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the OrderKart app > My Profile > Delete Account.
  • Web/Remote Request: Visit our Account Deletion Page or email supportorderkart@gmail.com.
  • Data Purged: Online login credentials, password hashes, active sessions, and FCM push tokens are permanently erased from our cloud database.
  • Statutory Retention: Past completed order invoices and physical store transaction ledgers are retained in the shop's local point-of-sale records as required by Indian commercial, taxation (GST), and accounting laws.
