Request Account & Data Deletion

Published in compliance with Google Play Store User Data & Account Deletion Policy.

Important Notice: Deleting your account will permanently remove your online mobile app login, session credentials, and push notification tokens from our cloud databases.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Fastest)

If you currently have the OrderKart application installed on your Android device:

  • Open the OrderKart app.
  • Tap on the My Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll down to the Information & Support section.
  • Tap Delete Account (highlighted in red) and confirm your choice.
  • Your online credentials will be purged immediately, and you will be signed out.

Method 2: Remote / Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit your registered customer details below or email us at supportorderkart@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Data Deletion & Retention Policy

What Data is Permanently Purged:
  • Online login credentials, password hashes, and security PINs.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens.
  • Active authentication session tokens.

Statutory Retention Exception: Under applicable Indian laws (including the Income Tax Act 1961, Goods & Services Tax (GST) Act 2017, and Commercial Record-keeping requirements), past completed transaction receipts, sales invoices, and customer ledger balances are legally retained in the store's offline administrative system for financial accounting and auditing purposes.

Turnaround Time: Web/Email deletion requests are verified and processed within 24 to 48 hours.
